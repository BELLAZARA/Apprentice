What the system does

Everything your farm needs, in one place

From the moment a rabbit is born to the day it is sold, every event is logged, searchable, and visible on your dashboard.

🐇
Animal Management

Each rabbit gets a unique profile with its full life history — breeding, weight, health, and sales all linked to one ID.

🧠
Decision Tracking

Record every management decision with its date, category, justification, and the person responsible. Log outcomes later to learn what works.

🌿
Feeding Records

Log feed type, quantity, and cage or block for every feeding session. Track monthly feed costs automatically.

💕
Breeding Events

Record male-female pairings, expected birth dates, kits born, and kits survived. Identify your best breeding pairs over time.

🩺
Health Monitoring

Log symptoms, treatments, medications, and outcomes per rabbit. Get alerts for vaccinations due and isolate sick animals quickly.

📈
Growth Tracking

Record regular weight measurements per rabbit. Compare growth rates across breeds and ages to optimise your feeding strategy.

💰
Sales & Revenue

Record every sale with buyer name, weight, price, and payment status. See your best and worst months at a glance.

📊
Reports & Analytics

Eight report types covering breeding, health, growth, sales, feed, mortality, and decision outcomes — ready to view anytime.

👥
Staff & Recruitment

Record hiring decisions, assign duties, and track whether staffing choices improved farm performance over time.
